# Darragh Griffin

I build small, focused apps for iPhone, Mac and the web. Each one does one job well.

Nine are in the works, launching one by one through late 2026.

## Apps

| App | What it does | Platform | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LiftAloud** | Workout tracker that logs your sets by voice, straight from the Lock Screen | iPhone | Release prep |
| **Hear My Pages** | A quiet library for your books that reads them aloud with on-device narration | iPhone | In build |

## How I work

- Native first: SwiftUI on Apple platforms, the web where it reaches people faster.
- Fair pricing: a useful free tier, then a simple subscription or a one-time price.
- Private by default: your data stays on your device wherever the app allows it.

## Code

Most of the source is private. Public repos hold what an app needs to fetch in the open, such as the [voice models for Hear My Pages](https://github.com/darraghgriffinn/hear-my-pages-voices).
